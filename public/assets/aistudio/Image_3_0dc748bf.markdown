## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the rare confluence of heritage viticulture, fine wine investment, and executive brand ambassadorship. Crafted for Guido Spina, the interface projects authoritative elegance, quiet luxury, and commercial precision. The aesthetic merges editorial wine journalism with executive-level portfolio analytics, addressing private collectors, Michelin-starred sommeliers, estate owners, and international luxury distributors.

The visual strategy relies on **Editorial Luxury Minimalism with Subtle Tactile Refinements**:
- Deep, cellar-aged burgundy depths anchor structural weight.
- Champagne gold accents lend celebratory prestige without ostentation.
- Sun-bleached limestone and warm parchment backgrounds evoke Mediterranean estates and tasting room stationery.
- Crisp hairline borders, measured typography scales, and discreet gold halations evoke high-grade physical labels and embossed vintage monographs.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from premier cru cellars, oak barrel aging, and gilded vintage foils.

- **Primary (`#5B1323`) & Deep Burgundy (`#430C18`)**: Used for primary brand markers, hero banners, statement buttons, and high-tier pedigree indicators. Communicates full-bodied depth, gravitas, and vintage heritage.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`) & Champagne Mist (`#DFBE82`)**: Employed selectively for metallic micro-accents, rating stars, badge strokes, active selection indicators, and delicate borders.
- **Dark Slate (`#1E1E24` and `#2D2D35`)**: The core typographic system color, deliberately chosen over pure black to provide a softer, ink-on-laid-paper warmth.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5`, `#F4EFEB`, `#FFFFFF`)**: Base canvases emulate premium uncoated paper and limestone terroir. `#FFFFFF` is reserved for elevated cards, highlighting purity against the `#FAF8F5` background wash.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear tension between classic editorial storytelling and sharp modern business data:

- **Playfair Display**: Conveys centuries of estate pedigree, prestige, and executive presence. Reserved for headlines, collection titles, wine appellations, and pull quotes.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: A clean, contemporary sans-serif engineered for digital legibility. Handles portfolio performance metrics, technical tasting notes, navigation, provenance data, and long-form narrative.
- **Uppercase Kerning**: Labels at the `label-md` and `label-sm` tiers mandate uppercase transformation paired with generous letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror luxury house catalog typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous, deliberate negative space reminiscent of broadsheet architectural reviews and collector monographs:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, `gutter-lg` (32px), `margin-lg` (80px), max container width of 1440px to ensure reading lines never overstretch.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, `gutter` (24px), `margin-md` (48px). Wine profile cards compress to dual-column formats.
- **Mobile (< 767px)**: 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (16px), `margin` (24px). Horizontal scroll containers are favored for vintage timelines, accolades, and cellar catalogs.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an 8px baseline matrix. Executive summary sections, hero arrangements, and portfolio showcases utilize expansive vertical cushions (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem-6rem in section wrappers) to preserve an unhurried, luxury aura.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy, synthetic drop shadows, relying instead on **low-contrast warm outlines paired with ambient, gold- and burgundy-kissed shadows**:

- **Ground Layer (Canvas)**: Tinted neutral `#FAF8F5` surface with no elevation.
- **Base Cards & Modules (Level 1)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` or pale parchment `#F4EFEB` resting over the canvas, defined by a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`. Ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive & Elevated Containers (Level 2)**: Hovered wine cards, modal sheets, and executive summary drawers. Ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(67, 12, 24, 0.08), 0 2px 8px 0 rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`.
- **Glow & Accent Elevation**: Curated selections and vintage ratings feature an inner champagne aura: `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px #C5A059, 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design uses **Soft (Level 1)** geometric tailoring. The corner language is restrained and architectural, rejecting overly playful bubble radii in favor of tailored corners found in bespoke stationery and luxury packaging:

- **Micro Components (Badges, Chips, Metadata Tags)**: 2px - 4px radius.
- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Input Fields)**: 4px (`0.25rem`) corner rounding for an authoritative, tailored profile.
- **Cards & Visual Containers**: 8px (`0.5rem`) radius maximum, keeping bottle photography and estate imagery crisp.
- **Avatars & Seal Emblems**: Geometric exceptions using strict 50% circular masks for wax seal emblems, ambassador portraiture, and sommelier pin badges.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Button**: Deep burgundy (`#5B1323`) background, crisp white label text, 1px champagne gold border (`#C5A059`). Hover state darkens to `#430C18` with an amplified gold glow (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Ambassador Action)**: Transparent background, dark slate text (`#1E1E24`), framed with a solid 1px champagne border (`#C5A059`). Hover shifts background to a delicate gold wash (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`).
- **Text Link**: Playfair Display italic or Plus Jakarta Sans uppercase with a permanent 1px underline positioned 4px below text, shifting color from slate to burgundy on interaction.

### Executive Wine Cards
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`). 
- Incorporates a dedicated vintage tag at the top right, bottle display area centered on an off-white tonal pedestal (`#FAF8F5`), followed by appellation, vintage year, critic scores, and ambassador tasting notes.
- Hover produces an elevation shift of -4px along the Y-axis and deepens the border to solid champagne gold.

### Badges & Terroir Chips
- Compact uppercase typography (`label-sm`), 4px corner radius.
- **Rating Badges**: Deep burgundy background with gold foil lettering (`#DFBE82`).
- **Classification Chips (e.g., DOCG, Grand Cru, Biodynamic)**: Neutral parchment (`#F4EFEB`) with a 1px stroke of `#C5A059` and slate text (`#2D2D35`).

### Form Inputs & Inquiries
- Tailored for high-net-worth acquisitions, cellar consultations, and ambassador booking.
- Background: `#FFFFFF` inset inside `#FAF8F5`. 
- Border: 1px subtle slate (`rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.15)`).
- Focus State: 1px solid `#5B1323` accompanied by a soft champagne focus ring (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`).
- Placeholder: Muted warm slate (`#2D2D35`, 50% opacity).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Checkbox elements feature sharp 2px corners, defaulting to an off-white fill and burgundy check glyph when selected.
- Segmented filter controls (e.g., "All Vintages", "Bordeaux", "Super Tuscan", "Library Stock") use an architectural pill or tab layout encased in a delicate gold-wire bounding frame.

### Specialized Portfolio Components
- **Tasting Radar & Scorecard**: Clean radial and linear gauge charts with wine-toned fills (`rgba(91, 19, 35, 0.15)`) and gold vertex lines for acid, tannin, oak, and aging potential.
- **Provenance Monogram Seal**: A subtle, vector wax-seal watermark incorporating Guido Spina’s executive monogram used as an authentication mark on private vintage releases.
